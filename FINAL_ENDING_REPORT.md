## 1. Cinematic Staging & Progression

When both characters step through their respective EXIT doors in Chamber 20, gameplay smoothly transitions into the **Grand Finale**:

### Sequence 1: The Approach (0.0s – 3.2s)
- The game screen renders the classic dual-world split: pristine white Real World on the left, deep obsidian Shadow World on the right, separated by the stark dividing line.
- Real character (black avatar) and Shadow character (white avatar) begin walking synchronously toward the center line.
- The atmospheric piano and cello ending theme swells (`soundEffects.playEndingTheme()`).

### Sequence 2: The Contact & Convergence Burst (3.2s – 5.0s)
- Both characters reach the exact dividing border and make contact.
- A brilliant radiant circular flash expands across the screen (scale factor 40x).
- The central dividing line permanently dissolves.
- The white and black backgrounds blend into a unified, harmonious twilight atmosphere (`#141416`).
- Real and Shadow merge into a single radiant beacon with an ambient breathing aura.

### Sequence 3: Title & Poetic Narrative Reveal (5.0s – 9.0s)
The screen unveils the iconic typography:

```
THE OTHER SIDE
TWO WORLDS. ONE MIND.

YOU WERE NEVER TRULY SEPARATE.
The light cast the shadow. The shadow defined the light.
Across twenty chambers of separation and reflection, the divide is closed.
```

### Sequence 4: Campaign Completion Record (9.0s+)
A sleek, bordered statistics card fades in with gold and silver accents:


## 2. Interactive Navigation Options

From the completion card, players are presented with three clear, responsive actions:
1. **REPLAY CAMPAIGN:** Immediately launches Chapter 1, Level 1 (`Awakening`), allowing speedrunners to replay the full 20-chamber arc.
2. **LEVEL SELECT:** Launches the interactive Level Selection grid, with all 20 levels unlocked and their best move counts recorded.
3. **MAIN MENU:** Returns to the atmospheric title screen with full settings, achievements, and credits access.
